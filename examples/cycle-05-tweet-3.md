# Cycle 05 — Tweet / "Twelve blind spots"

A tweet using the joke-lab methodology to create a funny announcement about the repo itself — this time highlighting the skeptical review that exposed the methodology's blind spots.

## Process

**Step 1 — Specific, real observation:** The joke-lab repo was reviewed by a Qwen 3.7 sub-agent. It identified 12 blind spots: contradictions between criteria, self-assessment bias, circular validation. The methodology had to be fixed based on feedback from an outsider — a skeptic who wasn't part of the system.

**Step 2 — Boring assumptions:** Generic brag ("I improved the joke system"), feature-list announcement ("now with 13 criteria"), or self-congratulation.

**Step 3 — Genuine surprise reversal:** The system gave itself straight As. It was wrong. The improvement came from the methodology being CHALLENGED, not from being followed.

**Step 4 — Misdirection with concrete details:** "Perfect scores" sounds like success → "audit the judge" reveals it was the grader that was broken → "12 issues" lands the twist.

**Step 5 — Punch word last:** "inside" — carries the entire blind-spot concept.

**Step 6 — Cut ruthlessly:** 6 lines. 26 words. No filler.

**Step 7 — Edge check:** Self-deprecating. Honest. Sharp without being mean.
